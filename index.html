About Me

A young twist on classic magic

I'm Alex, a 14-year-old magician from Cambridgeshire. I picked up my first deck of cards in 2019 and never really put it down.

Since then, with a lot of practice and some great mentors, I've gone from basic card tricks to full performance routines. My style is modern and funny, and I try to keep it interesting for everyone in the room. I'm a member of the Magic Circle youth initiative and The Cambridge Pentacle Club.

I know how to hold kids' attention, but I also build in the kind of sleight of hand that catches adults off guard. At a family party, a wedding, or a bigger event, my aim is the same: give people something they'll actually talk about afterwards.
